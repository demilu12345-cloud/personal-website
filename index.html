关于我

About me

你好，我是陆晓，目前在中国科学院理论物理研究所担任助理研究员，从事原子核理论研究。

我的工作聚焦于投影后变分壳模型的发展与应用，以及原子核中的八极关联、手征对称性破缺和形变晕核的软偶极响应。

我于 2023 年在中国原子能科学研究院获得博士学位，随后在中国科学院理论物理研究所从事博士后研究，并于 2025 年起担任助理研究员。

Welcome! I am Xiao Lu, an assistant researcher at the Institute of Theoretical Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, working on nuclear theory.

My research focuses on the development and application of the variation-after-projection shell model, octupole correlations and chiral symmetry breaking in nuclei, and the soft dipole response of deformed halo nuclei.

I received my Ph.D. from the China Institute of Atomic Energy in 2023. I then joined the Institute of Theoretical Physics, CAS, as a postdoctoral researcher and became an assistant researcher in 2025.
